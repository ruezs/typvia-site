隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 24 日

Typvia 把你保存的文字放在你自己的设备上。我们不需要注册，不放广告，不做统计，看不到你保存的内容。

一句话版本：不同步，什么都不会离开你的设备。打开同步后，内容会先在你的设备上加密再上传，服务器只能存，读不了。用 AI 时，只有你点了「发送」的那一条正文，会发给你自己添加的 AI 服务。

1. 存在你设备上的内容

  • 你保存的片段、分类、触发词、使用次数和版本历史，存放在设备本地的数据库里。
  • 保险库里的内容用你的主密码加密（Argon2id 派生密钥，XChaCha20-Poly1305 加密）。主密码和密钥不会写进数据库，也不会上传。密钥只存放在系统的安全存储里（Keychain / Android Keystore / Windows DPAPI）。
  • 标记为机密的内容不会进入搜索索引、日志、Espanso 配置和 AI 请求。

2. 同步（可选）

同步默认关闭。你可以选择以下三种方式之一：Typvia 提供的同步服务、你自己部署的同步服务器，或者你自己的 WebDAV 存储。

同步的内容在你的设备上加密，加密后才上传。解密所需的密钥只在你配对过的设备上，服务器没有。

使用 Typvia 提供的同步服务时，服务器上会保存：

内容服务器能否读到
片段、分类、设置等同步记录不能，只存密文
每条记录的类型、版本号、更新时间、来自哪台设备能。这些是排序和合并时需要的
设备名称（如「iPhone 15 Pro」）和平台（iOS / Android / macOS / Windows）能。用于在你的设备列表里显示
设备公钥、证书和撤销记录能。这些本身是公开信息，用于验证设备身份
恢复数据不能，它用你的恢复码加密
配对邀请不能读到配对码，只存它的摘要，几分钟后过期

同步服务通过 Cloudflare 的网络提供。和访问任何网站一样，请求经过时会带上你的 IP 地址。我们的服务端程序不记录 IP，日志里只有错误类别，没有任何内容。

暂存（跨设备临时传文字）的条目同样加密传输，24 小时后自动删除。

删除：在设备上退出账户，会把这台设备从账户中移除。如果要删除账户在服务器上的全部数据，请通过支持页联系我们。

3. AI 功能（可选）

  • AI 功能默认不可用。你需要先自己添加一个 AI 服务（填地址、模型和 API Key），或者在支持的 iPhone 上使用系统自带的端侧模型。
  • 只有你选好动作并点了「发送」，那一条片段的正文才会发给你添加的服务。分类和其他片段都不会发。机密内容和疑似密钥的文字不会发送。
  • 请求是从你的设备直接发给该服务，不经过我们。之后的处理遵循该服务自己的隐私政策。
  • API Key 存放在系统安全存储里，不会同步，也不会写进日志。
  • 使用端侧模型时，文字不会离开设备。

4. 键盘与输入法

  • iOS 键盘不申请「完全访问」，没有联网能力，只读取 Typvia 为它准备的片段列表。
  • 键盘和输入法不记录你输入的内容，不保存按键历史，也不读取与插入片段无关的输入内容。

5. 剪贴板

  • 只有你主动操作时（点复制、按暂存快捷键、点「存为片段」），Typvia 才会读写剪贴板。
  • 电脑上「复制的文字自动暂存」默认关闭。打开后，Typvia 会把你复制的文字送到你的其他设备，但会跳过密码管理器标记为不记录的内容和疑似密钥的文字，也不保存剪贴板历史。
  • 复制机密内容后，剪贴板会在 30 秒后自动清除。

6. 浏览器扩展

扩展只和你电脑上的 Typvia 应用通信，不连接任何服务器，也不读取你没有选中的网页内容。

7. 我们不做的事

  • 不需要邮箱、手机号或任何注册信息。
  • 没有广告，没有第三方统计或追踪，没有崩溃上报。
  • 不出售、不出租、不共享你的任何数据。

8. 第三方

  • Cloudflare：承载 Typvia 提供的同步服务的网络。
  • 你添加的 AI 服务、你使用的 WebDAV 存储：由你选择，遵循其各自的隐私政策。
  • App Store / Google Play：下载和更新应用时，适用其各自的隐私政策。

9. 儿童

Typvia 不面向 13 岁以下的儿童，也不会有意收集儿童的信息。

10. 政策变更

如果这份政策有实质变化，我们会更新本页顶部的生效日期，并在应用的版本说明中告知。

11. 联系我们

关于隐私的任何问题，请通过支持页上的方式联系我们。

Privacy Policy

Effective November 24, 2026

Typvia keeps what you save on your own devices. There is no sign-up, no ads and no analytics, and we can't see what you save.

In short: without sync, nothing leaves your device. With sync on, content is encrypted on your device before it is uploaded, so the server can store it but can't read it. When you use AI, only the text of the one snippet you choose to send goes to the AI service you added yourself.

1. What stays on your device

  • Your snippets, categories, triggers, usage counts and version history are kept in a database on the device.
  • Vault items are encrypted with your master password (key derived with Argon2id, encrypted with XChaCha20-Poly1305). The master password and keys are never written to the database or uploaded. Keys are kept only in the system's secure storage (Keychain / Android Keystore / Windows DPAPI).
  • Anything marked as secret is kept out of the search index, logs, Espanso configuration and AI requests.

2. Sync (optional)

Sync is off by default. You can use the sync service Typvia provides, a sync server you run yourself, or your own WebDAV storage.

What is synced is encrypted on your device before it is uploaded. The keys needed to read it exist only on devices you have paired; the server doesn't have them.

With the sync service Typvia provides, the server keeps:

WhatCan the server read it?
Synced records: snippets, categories, settingsNo. Only ciphertext is stored
Each record's type, version number, update time and the device it came fromYes. These are needed to order and merge changes
Device name (such as "iPhone 15 Pro") and platform (iOS / Android / macOS / Windows)Yes. Used to show your list of devices
Device public keys, certificates and removal recordsYes. These are public by nature and prove which devices belong to you
Recovery dataNo. It is encrypted with your recovery code
Pairing invitationsNo. Only a digest of the code is stored, and it expires within minutes

The sync service is delivered through Cloudflare's network. As with any website, requests carry your IP address on the way. Our server software doesn't record IP addresses, and its logs contain only error categories, never content.

Hold items (text sent between your devices for a short time) are encrypted the same way and deleted after 24 hours.

Deleting: signing out of the account on a device removes that device from the account. To delete all of an account's data from the server, contact us through the support page.

3. AI (optional)

  • AI is not available until you add an AI service yourself (address, model and API key), or use the on-device model on a supported iPhone.
  • Only when you pick an action and tap Send is the text of that one snippet sent to the service you added. Categories and other snippets are not sent. Secret items and text that looks like a credential are never sent.
  • The request goes from your device straight to that service, not through us. From there, that service's own privacy policy applies.
  • API keys are kept in the system's secure storage. They are not synced and never written to logs.
  • With the on-device model, the text never leaves the device.

4. Keyboard

  • The iOS keyboard doesn't ask for Full Access and has no network access. It reads only the snippet list Typvia prepares for it.
  • The keyboards on iOS and Android don't record what you type, keep no keystroke history, and don't read input beyond what inserting a snippet needs.

5. Clipboard

  • Typvia reads or writes the clipboard only when you ask it to: tapping Copy, pressing the Hold shortcut, or saving a selection as a snippet.
  • On the computer, sending what you copy to Hold is off by default. When you turn it on, text you copy is sent to your other devices, except content a password manager marks as not to be recorded and text that looks like a credential. No clipboard history is kept.
  • After you copy a secret item, the clipboard is cleared after 30 seconds.

6. Browser extension

The extension talks only to the Typvia app on your computer. It connects to no server and doesn't read page content you haven't selected.

7. What we don't do

  • We don't ask for an email address, phone number or any sign-up details.
  • No ads, no third-party analytics or tracking, no crash reporting.
  • We don't sell, rent or share any of your data.

8. Third parties

  • Cloudflare: the network that carries the sync service Typvia provides.
  • The AI service and WebDAV storage you add: chosen by you, under their own privacy policies.
  • App Store / Google Play: their own privacy policies apply when you download and update the app.

9. Children

Typvia is not directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect information from them.

10. Changes

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, we will update the effective date at the top of this page and mention it in the app's release notes.

11. Contact

For any privacy question, reach us through the support page.
