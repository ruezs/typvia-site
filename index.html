Typvia 支持

存一次，到处都能打出来。常用的文字存进 Typvia，在任何应用里用触发词或键盘调出来。

常见问题

怎么在 iPhone 上打开 Typvia 键盘？

打开「设置 → 通用 → 键盘 → 键盘 → 添加新键盘」，选择 Typvia。之后在任意输入框里点地球键切换到 Typvia。Typvia 键盘不需要「完全访问」。

怎么在 Android 上打开 Typvia 输入法？

打开系统设置中的「语言和输入法 → 屏幕键盘 / 管理键盘」，启用 Typvia，然后在输入时切换到它。不同品牌手机的菜单名称略有不同。

Mac 上触发词没有展开？

Typvia 需要「辅助功能」权限才能把文字填进其他应用。打开「系统设置 → 隐私与安全性 → 辅助功能」，确认 Typvia 已开启。如果是更新后失效，把它关掉再打开一次。

怎么让几台设备同步？

在第一台设备上打开同步并创建账户。在要加入的设备上选择加入，按提示输入另一台设备上显示的码。两边会显示一串核对码，确认一致后再完成。同步的内容只有你的设备能看到。

同步有点慢？

保存后一般一两秒内就会出现在其他设备上。手机在后台时系统会暂停同步，回到前台会立即同步。如果你的网络经过代理或跨境线路，第一次连接可能要多等几秒。

什么是「暂存」？

把一段文字临时送到你的其他设备，比如电脑上复制、手机上取用。暂存的内容同样加密，24 小时后自动删除。

忘记了保险库的主密码？

主密码无法找回。我们没有你的密钥，也无法替你解密；忘记主密码后，保险库里的内容就无法再打开，普通片段不受影响。

换手机或不用了，怎么处理数据？

在旧设备上退出账户，它会从账户中移除。如果要删除账户在服务器上的全部数据，请发邮件告诉我们，并附上其中一台设备上显示的设备名。

可以自己部署同步服务器吗？

可以。同步服务端是开源的，也可以改用你自己的 WebDAV 存储。

Typvia Support

Save once. Type anywhere. Keep the text you use often in Typvia, and bring it up in any app with a trigger or the keyboard.

Questions

How do I turn on the Typvia keyboard on iPhone?

Go to Settings → General → Keyboard → Keyboards → Add New Keyboard and choose Typvia. Then tap the globe key in any text field to switch to it. The Typvia keyboard doesn't need Full Access.

How do I turn on the Typvia keyboard on Android?

In system settings, open Languages & input → On-screen keyboard (or Manage keyboards), turn on Typvia, and switch to it while typing. Menu names vary a little between phone makers.

Triggers don't expand on my Mac.

Typvia needs Accessibility permission to type into other apps. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility and make sure Typvia is on. If it stopped after an update, turn it off and on again.

How do I sync my devices?

Turn on sync on your first device and create an account. On the device you want to add, choose to join and enter the code shown on the other device. Both show a check code; finish only when they match. What you sync can be read only on your devices.

Sync feels slow.

A save usually shows up on your other devices within a second or two. Phones pause sync in the background and catch up as soon as you open the app. If your connection goes through a proxy or a long international route, the first connection can take a few seconds more.

What is Hold?

A way to send a piece of text to your other devices for a short time, such as copying on your computer and using it on your phone. Held items are encrypted the same way and deleted after 24 hours.

I forgot my vault's master password.

The master password can't be recovered. We don't have your keys and can't decrypt anything for you. Without it, the vault can't be opened again; your other snippets are unaffected.

I'm changing phones or stopping. What happens to my data?

Sign out of the account on the old device and it is removed from the account. To delete all of the account's data from the server, email us with the device name shown on one of your devices.

Can I run my own sync server?

Yes. The sync server is open source, and you can also use your own WebDAV storage instead.
